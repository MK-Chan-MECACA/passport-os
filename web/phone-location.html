Passport · Phone location

Let your phone
find your city.

Tap the button below and allow the browser to use your location. Back on the setup page, the nearest city is matched. Check it, then save.

If it does not go back by itself, copy the result, switch to the device setup page and paste it. Your phone must be on the device hotspot to save.

This page does not send your coordinates to any server and does not track you. Your location only reaches the device setup page when you tap Send or copy it, and you confirm before saving.
